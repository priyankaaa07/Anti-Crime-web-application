Welcome to Jan Saathi

Jan Saathi is a revolutionary app designed to empower every Indian with digital tools for everyday life. Our mission is to make technology accessible, useful, and meaningful for everyone, from metropolitan cities to rural villages.

With over 2 million users across India, Jan Saathi has become the trusted digital companion for navigating daily challenges, accessing government services, and connecting with local communities.

Our team of passionate developers, designers, and community experts work tirelessly to create solutions that are intuitive in Hindi, English, and 10 other regional languages, ensuring no one is left behind in India's digital revolution.

Our Vision

We envision a India where every citizen, regardless of their technical proficiency or location, can harness the power of digital technology to improve their lives, access opportunities, and participate fully in the digital economy.

Jan Saathi aims to break down barriers to technology adoption by creating solutions that are culturally relevant, linguistically appropriate, and designed for the unique needs of Indian users.

"To empower 100 million Indians with digital accessibility by 2025, creating a more inclusive and connected nation."

This vision drives our product development, community initiatives, and partnerships with government organizations and NGOs across India.

Our Mission

Our mission is to democratize digital technology by creating an all-in-one platform that simplifies everyday tasks, provides access to essential services, and builds digital literacy among all segments of Indian society.

We are committed to understanding the unique challenges faced by Indians in adopting technology and developing tailored solutions that address these barriers while preserving the richness of our diverse cultures.

Simplify

Make complex digital tasks simple and accessible for everyone

Educate

Build digital literacy through intuitive design and guidance

Empower

Give every Indian the tools to thrive in the digital era

Our Objectives

To fulfill our mission and progress toward our vision, we've established clear objectives that guide our development and community initiatives:

Language Accessibility

Support all 22 scheduled languages of India by 2024 to ensure no language barrier prevents digital access.

User Growth

Reach 10 million active users by 2024, with at least 40% from rural areas and 50% women users.

Feature Development

Launch 5 new features annually that address the most pressing needs of Indian users based on continuous feedback.

Partnerships

Establish partnerships with all state governments to integrate essential services into Jan Saathi by 2025.
