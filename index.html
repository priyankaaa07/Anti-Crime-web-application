    Services

    At Suraksha Sathi, we provide guidance on personal safety, crime prevention, emergency support, and incident reporting. Our goal is to empower users with the knowledge and tools needed to stay safe and protected.

    Crime Reporting System

    Users can anonymously report crimes with details like location, time, and description. Option to attach images or voice notes.

    Emergency SOS Alert

    A one-tap SOS button that sends live location to emergency contacts or nearby authorities instantly.

    Nearby Police & Helpline Finder

    Shows nearest police stations, women helplines, cybercrime cells, and emergency contacts based on user location.

    AI Chat Assistant for Safety Advice

    Users can ask questions like “What should I do if I get harassed?” and get instant safety instructions.

    Safety Tips & Awareness Feed

    Daily updated tips about cyber safety, street safety, women safety, and fraud prevention.

    Nearby Safety Map

    Helps you quickly locate police stations, hospitals, and emergency support centers during urgent situations.

    Emergency Quick Links Hub

    Provides instant access to verified emergency services and government safety portals. Designed to save time by giving one-click access.

    Safety Tips & Awareness Feed

    Daily updated tips about cyber safety, street safety, women safety, and fraud prevention.
