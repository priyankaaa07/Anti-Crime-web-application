Should You Add a "Meet My Team" Section?

Analyzing the pros and cons for your Jan Saathi app

Considerations for Your App

Pros of Adding a Team Section

  • Builds trust and credibility with your users
  • Personalizes your app and creates emotional connections
  • Showcases expertise and experience behind your product
  • Helps with recruiting if you're looking to grow your team
  • Can differentiate your app from competitors

Cons of Adding a Team Section

  • Takes up valuable screen real estate
  • Requires ongoing maintenance as team changes
  • Might not align with a minimalist app design
  • Could be perceived as self-promotional
  • May not be relevant to all users

Recommendation for Jan Saathi

Since Jan Saathi is a community-focused app, a team section could help build trust and show the people behind the platform. However, consider placing it in an "About" section rather than the main navigation to avoid clutter. Focus on highlighting team members' passion for community building rather than just their credentials.
