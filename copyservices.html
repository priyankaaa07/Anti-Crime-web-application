Services

At Jan Saathi, we offer a range of services designed to empower citizens and improve the quality of life in their communities.

Road & Infrastructure

Report potholes, broken streets, damaged footpaths, and missing road signs to keep your community safe.

Water Supply Issues

Notify authorities about water leaks, contaminated water supply, low water pressure, and drainage problems.

Electricity Problems

Report power outages, faulty street lights, exposed electrical wires, and other electricity-related issues.

Waste Management

Report garbage accumulation, missed trash collections, overflowing bins, and illegal dumping sites.

Parks & Public Spaces

Notify about maintenance issues in parks, damaged public property, and need for beautification.

Public Safety Concerns

Report unsafe public areas, broken manholes, dangerous buildings, and other public safety hazards.

Transport Issues

Report problems with public transport, damaged bus stops, traffic signal issues, and road congestion points.

Community Support

Connect with local volunteers, report issues requiring community action, and join neighborhood initiatives.
